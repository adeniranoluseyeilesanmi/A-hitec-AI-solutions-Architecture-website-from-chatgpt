Frequently asked questions

Do we need to replace our current systems?

No. The first step is to review the tools and process you already use. Any integration depends on access, permissions, technical feasibility and the agreed scope.

Can you work with confidential financial information?

We first map the data and assess whether the proposed workflow can be tested using synthetic, redacted or otherwise appropriately authorised data. Production use requires agreed security, privacy, supplier and access controls. Do not send confidential client information through this website.

Will the AI make decisions for our staff?

The proposed service is designed to assist staff, not replace accountable human judgement. Any consequential action needs an explicit approval path and an agreed risk assessment.

How long does a project take?

A discovery sprint is typically planned over several working days, depending on availability. A pilot timeline is agreed only after scope, integrations and acceptance tests are understood.

Do you guarantee savings or compliance?

No. Results depend on the workflow, data, usage and adoption. We agree measurable acceptance criteria for each scoped project, but do not guarantee financial returns, regulatory compliance or error-free AI.

What happens after the pilot?

If the pilot is useful, we can discuss a support plan or a repeatable subscription product. Ongoing hosting, monitoring and changes are priced separately and should have clear ownership.
