ANSWERS

Questions, answered.

Clear expectations before you commit.

What if AI is not the right solution?+

We will say so. The assessment is designed to identify whether an existing tool, process change or AI solution is justified.

How much does implementation cost?+

We define the workflow, integrations, controls and acceptance criteria first. A fixed-price proposal follows; software and usage charges may be separate.

Will our data be secure?+

Security, access, data handling and human review are considered during design. Appropriate safeguards depend on the systems and information involved.

Will AI replace our staff?+

The initial focus is reducing repetitive administration and supporting employees, with people retaining approval for important decisions.

How quickly can we start?+

A short discovery call establishes fit. Delivery dates depend on scope, access to systems and client availability.
