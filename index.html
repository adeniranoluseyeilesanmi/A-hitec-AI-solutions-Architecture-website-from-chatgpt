Frequently asked questions

Questions, answered

Does my business need AI?

Not necessarily. We first assess the business challenge and compare AI with simpler process or software improvements.

Can you work with our existing systems?

We assess available integrations, data access, technical constraints and security requirements before recommending an approach.

How long does an AI project take?

Timelines depend on scope, data quality, integrations and approval requirements. We define milestones during discovery.

How do you handle sensitive business data?

We assess data sensitivity, access controls, privacy obligations and human oversight as part of the proposed architecture.
